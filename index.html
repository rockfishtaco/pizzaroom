Our Story

Founded in 2010, Slice Heaven started as a small family-owned pizzeria with a passion for authentic Italian flavors. We use only the freshest ingredients, locally sourced when possible, and our dough is made fresh daily.

Every pizza is handcrafted with care and baked to perfection in our stone-fired oven.
